The full benchmark report reads best as a single page: the Terminal-Bench floor (why this harness gives
Token Optimizer only one of its six levers), the controlled per-mechanism A/B, the safety suite, and one
real month of savings — the controlled and real-world numbers kept honestly separate, every figure with
its interval.

<LinkCard title="Open the benchmark report →" href="/benchmarks.html" description="Terminal-Bench floor, controlled A/B, safety suite, and one real month — with intervals, not point estimates." />

:::note
Two kinds of number live in the report and are never added together or compared as one claim. The
controlled benchmark tells you the least to expect from one mechanism; the real month shows what a full
setup looked like for one person. Reproduce the parts that touch your own data with
`python3 scripts/measure.py savings` and `python3 scripts/benchmark.py`.
:::
